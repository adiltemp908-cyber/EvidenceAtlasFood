## Collection and screening

PubMed subject-heading searches and Europe PMC title/abstract searches targeted human dietary contexts through November 4. The initial pass covered all eleven topics in the recent publication stratum and continued older searches for water and fruit/vegetable intake. A complementary pass across 2000–2026 added review, guideline, recommendation and reference-value queries for all eleven topics. It was a bounded collection pass, not an exhaustive literature search. Query cursors and provider responses are retained locally.

The acquisition returned 189 new records; 48 remain outside search after provisional scope checks. Metadata and raw sources are preserved. Screening held animal/veterinary material, contamination studies, protocols and identified industrial or perception-only papers for review. Unknown MeSH species were not automatically excluded when the title/abstract established human context. These are rule-based and AI-assisted screening decisions, not independent expert judgments.

## Activation and checks

Incremental embedding reused **109,628 existing vectors** and encoded **6,255 new vectors**. The original corpus/index pair and a pre-review snapshot are retained locally. Publication preserved **19 saved investigations**.

Thirty-six collection, core retrieval and incremental/chunked-index regression tests passed. SQLite integrity, paper identifiers and source relationships were checked. Six live queries and 82 exact source passages across 29 papers were verified after activation; details are in [measured checks](checks/coverage-2026-11-04.json). The original water question now retrieves human plain-water studies and the daily-water-requirement review in its top five. Some other everyday queries still rank tangential studies highly. Saved before/after rankings are development diagnostics and have no independently judged accuracy score. Retrieval and reranker algorithms were not changed by this expansion.
