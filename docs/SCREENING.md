# Domain eligibility v1

Include studies of food/beverages, dietary patterns, nutrients, preparation, nutrition supplements, and food exposures with substantive human-health, nutrient-status, performance, sleep or safety relevance. Retain useful animal/laboratory evidence with explicit labels. Do not infer human evidence simply because a paper mentions health.

Exclude farming yield, livestock feed, packaging engineering and industrial optimization unless human nutrition/health relevance is substantively present. Metadata rules are provisional and favor a review queue over irreversible exclusion. Keep every decision/reason/query and raw metadata. No selection by supportive findings, citations, impact factor or OA availability.

Screen title/abstract and MeSH. Distinguish accepted, excluded and needs-review. Inspect a topic-stratified sample of accepted, excluded and unresolved records; human review still required for validated screening performance. Document false positives/negatives, denominator, reviewer and changed rules. A mechanistic paper must never be described as demonstrated human effects.

OA availability affects depth, not metadata eligibility. Missing full text is a coverage limitation. Reviews and included primary studies are not independent votes. Identifier deduplication does not establish study-cohort independence; overlap remains unresolved unless documented.
